# Trigger map

Which phrases each skill owns, and where it hands off. Generated from the skill descriptions; `tools/check.py` fails if two skills claim the same phrase.

When a user's wording could fit two skills, the 'Not for' column says who wins. Routing is tested against `evals/routing-prompts.tsv` by giving a blind router only the descriptions and comparing its choices to the expected skill.

| Skill | Owns these phrases | Not for |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `account-context` | account context; set up my context; teach you my business; my skills feel generic; what does healthy look like for us; Account context | researching one customer (use handoff) or planning an account (use account-plan). |
| `account-plan` | account plan; strategic account plan; twelve month plan; what is our plan for [account]; plan for this account; write up the account strategy | the first 90 days of a new customer (use onboarding-plan), a renewal call (use renewal-risk) or choosing which accounts earn a plan (use book-triage). |
| `account-review` | account on a page; monthly account review; account review for; one page on [account]; what changed on this account since last month; run my monthly review | the renewal call (use renewal-risk), the customer meeting (use business-review) or a twelve month strategy (use account-plan). |
| `adoption-plan` | low adoption; they are not using it; usage has stalled; shelfware; seats sitting idle; adoption plan | a new customer still onboarding (use onboarding-plan), the renewal call (use renewal-risk) or an expansion pitch (use expansion-case). |
| `advocacy-ask` | reference call; case study; testimonial; sales wants a reference; can we use them as a logo; who can we ask for a review; G2 review | proving the value that justifies the ask (use business-review) or trading a reference in a negotiation (use renewal-negotiation). |
| `book-review` | review my whole book; quarterly book review; concentration risk in my book; renewal cliff; my book on a page; where is my book exposed | choosing this week's accounts (use book-triage) or checking a team's reads (use portfolio-review). |
| `book-triage` | triage my book; which accounts this week; plan my week; my book is too big; who needs me this week; I cannot cover them all | the depth read on one account (use renewal-risk) or for designing who gets a CSM (use coverage-model). |
| `business-review` | QBR; EBR; should I run a QBR; prep the business review; build the review deck; what should I present to [account]; exec review next week | deciding if the account will renew (use renewal-risk) or for a 15-minute exec slot (use exec-conversation). |
| `call-prep` | prep me for my call with; call prep; I have a call in an hour; what should I ask them; talking points for; walking into this call cold | a QBR (use business-review), a senior-executive meeting (use exec-conversation) or delivering bad news (use hard-conversation). |
| `call-recap` | recap this call; here is the transcript; what do I owe after that call; write the follow-ups; emails from this call; what did you make of that call | preparing a call (use call-prep) or polishing an email already written (use email-critic). |
| `champion-loss` | my champion left; sponsor left; our contact changed roles; my sponsor went dark; new owner on the account; they deactivated their account | mapping a healthy account (use stakeholder-map) or the renewal call (use renewal-risk). |
| `churn-postmortem` | churn postmortem; why did we lose them; they did not renew; post mortem on [account]; what went wrong with [account]; lessons learned from the loss | an account still alive (use renewal-risk) or for running the exit conversation (use offboarding). |
| `coaching` | coach my CSM; feedback on this call; call review; my CSM keeps making the same mistake; how do I give feedback on | calibrating account reads across a team (use portfolio-review) or writing the customer recap (use call-recap). |
| `commitments` | commitments ledger; who promised what; what did we agree to; what are they on the hook for; pull the promises out of this thread; log the action items from this call | the follow-up emails after a call (use call-recap) or a promise handover to a successor (use handoff). |
| `coverage-model` | coverage model; CSM ratio; how many accounts per CSM; do we need more headcount; high touch low touch; we cannot cover the long tail | working a book that is already tiered (use book-triage) or what coverage implies for the forecast (use renewal-forecast). |
| `cs-scorecard` | CS scorecard; CS metrics; what should we measure; KPIs for my book; how do I report on my team; define our retention metrics | the board retention story (use nrr-narrative) or auditing a health model (use health-score). |
| `cs-start` | customer success; help with my accounts; where do I start; what can you do for a CSM; I'm a CSM; I lead a CS team; run the demo; show me what this does | doing the work itself (the routed skill does that). |
| `csm-hiring` | interview kit for a CSM; hiring a CSM; CSM interview questions; work sample for a CSM candidate; scorecard for the CSM role; how do I tell a good CSM from a nice one | onboarding the hire (use csm-ramp) or coaching a current CSM (use coaching). |
| `csm-ramp` | ramp plan for a new CSM; new CSM starts Monday; 30 60 90 for my new hire; is my new CSM ramped; what should my new CSM do in week one; inherited a book as a new CSM | a customer's first 90 days (use onboarding-plan) or one account's handover (use handoff). |
| `csql-motion` | CSQL; CS sourced pipeline; should CS sell; hand expansion leads to sales; CS to sales handoff for expansion; how do we credit CS for expansion | the business case on one deal (use expansion-case) or the team's capacity (use coverage-model). |
| `customer-update` | customer update; notify customers; announcement to customers; end of life notice; maintenance window; outage notice; tell them about the delay | bad news to one account in a live conversation (use hard-conversation) or an account already on fire (use escalation). |
| `day-plan` | plan my day; build my day around these meetings; when do I do the recaps; I have back to back calls today; fit my replies around my calendar; shut down time | choosing which accounts get time this week (use book-triage) or ranking what you owe (use what-do-i-owe). |
| `email-critic` | would you send this; review this email; tighten this; does this sound right; how will the customer take this | turning a recording into emails (use call-recap) or a message to a whole list (use customer-update). |
| `escalation` | their VP is involved; the account is on fire; angry customer escalation; they are threatening to churn; they want a call today; this has gone to their exec | bad news not yet delivered (use hard-conversation) or an account that has already left. |
| `exec-conversation` | I have fifteen minutes with their CFO; meeting my champion's boss; exec sponsor meeting; new VP just joined; what do I say to a C-level | a QBR (use business-review) or when the point of the meeting is bad news (use hard-conversation). |
| `expansion-case` | expansion case; they want to add seats; second department; build the business case for; how do I get budget for; my champion needs to sell this internally; upsell | the review meeting (use business-review) or price haggling (use renewal-negotiation). |
| `expansion-scan` | scan my book for expansion; where is the upsell hiding; who is ready to expand; rank my expansion opportunities; which accounts are outgrowing their plan; seats are nearly full | the business case on one deal (use expansion-case) or passing a lead to sales (use csql-motion). |
| `handoff` | handoff; I am leaving this account; hand over my accounts; I just inherited this account; taking over the account; get me up to speed on this account | the message to the customer (use customer-update) or a customer who is leaving (use offboarding). |
| `hard-conversation` | how do I tell them; I have to tell the customer; we missed the date; I have been putting this off; they are going to be angry; how do I break this to | an escalation already under way (use escalation) or a meeting whose point is a senior customer executive (use exec-conversation). |
| `health-score` | audit our health score; the score said green but they churned; build a health score; is our health score any good; why is this account red; backtest the score | one account's renewal call (use renewal-risk) or book-level coverage (use coverage-model). |
| `internal-ask` | feature request; raise this with product; I need engineering to look at this; get leadership involved; I need an exception; nobody is picking this up; how do I get this prioritized | a customer escalating to you (use escalation) or news for a customer (use hard-conversation). |
| `invoice-chase` | overdue invoice; chase the invoice; they have not paid; the PO is stuck; AP has not paid us; payment reminder | a customer asking for a discount (use renewal-negotiation) or a billing dispute that has become an escalation (use escalation). |
| `mutual-action-plan` | mutual action plan; shared plan with the customer; joint plan with dates; customer-facing timeline; owners on both sides; they keep missing their dates | the internal readiness call and watchlist (use onboarding-plan) or a twelve month internal strategy (use account-plan). |
| `nrr-narrative` | NRR; net revenue retention; why did NRR drop; retention slide for the board; explain our retention numbers; what is our NRR hiding | the forward number (use renewal-forecast) or the account reads underneath it (use portfolio-review). |
| `offboarding` | offboarding; they gave notice; they have decided to leave; wind down the account; exit conversation; data export request; how do I handle a clean exit | saving an account still in play (use renewal-risk) or the internal analysis afterward (use churn-postmortem). |
| `onboarding-plan` | onboarding plan; just closed a new customer; kickoff is next week; first 90 days; time to first value; is this customer really live | the sales handover itself (use handoff) or the long-run account plan (use account-plan). |
| `one-pager` | one pager; something they can forward; my sponsor needs to send this up; summary for their boss; leave-behind; brief for their leadership | a budget case for more scope (use expansion-case), the review meeting itself (use business-review) or a list broadcast (use customer-update). |
| `playbook-builder` | build a playbook; turn this into a play; we keep handling this differently; write the play for; make this repeatable; standardize our response to | one account's plan (use account-plan) or the message announcing a change to customers (use customer-update). |
| `portfolio-review` | portfolio review; review my team's reads; everything is green and I do not believe it; someone is sandbagging; calibrate my team; how do I run the forecast call | the forecast number itself (use renewal-forecast) or one-to-one development (use coaching). |
| `price-increase` | price increase; renewal uplift; raise prices on my book; which accounts do we protect; notice period for the increase; price rise letter | the live conversation with one customer (use hard-conversation) or a broadcast about a changed plan (use customer-update). |
| `renewal-forecast` | renewal forecast; forecast the quarter; what will we renew; commit number; GRR forecast; roll up my team's renewals | one account's read (use renewal-risk) or explaining past retention to a board (use nrr-narrative). |
| `renewal-negotiation` | they want a discount; procurement is involved; how much can I give; they want to cut seats; competitor quoted us half; prep the renewal negotiation; they are asking for utilization data | deciding if they will renew (use renewal-risk) or delivering a price increase (use hard-conversation). |
| `renewal-risk` | will they renew; is this account at risk; renewal risk; churn risk; should I be worried about [account]; what's the read on [account]; they asked for our utilization numbers or the notice date | scoring a health model (use health-score), planning the timeline (use renewal-runway) or a champion who just left (use champion-loss). |
| `renewal-runway` | renewal runway; renewal timeline; when do I start the renewal; plan backward from the renewal date; I am late on this renewal; opt-out date | judging the account (use renewal-risk) or negotiating terms (use renewal-negotiation). |
| `sales-handoff` | closed-won handoff; the AE just closed; sales handoff; what did sales promise; intake from the AE; questions for the AE | CSM to CSM or leaving an account (use handoff) or the full onboarding schedule (use onboarding-plan). |
| `save-plan` | save plan; save this account; can we still save them; 30 day rescue; they are leaning toward leaving; turnaround plan for | judging whether they will renew (use renewal-risk), a customer who already gave notice (use offboarding) or a live angry escalation (use escalation). |
