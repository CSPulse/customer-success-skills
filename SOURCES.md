# Sources

What the skills draw on, and how much weight each source can bear. Most customer success writing is vendor or practitioner opinion, not research, so the skills are built on mechanisms (why something is true) rather than numbers. Where a skill gives a timeline or threshold, it is labeled "a common default, set your own".

Evidence grades: **Primary** (the originator's own writing), **Practitioner** (a named practitioner's or vendor's playbook, directional), **Survey** (self-reported, sample stated), **Unverified** (appears in several places with no traceable study; not used as a basis).

| Topic | Source | Grade | Used for |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Desired outcome (required outcome plus appropriate experience) | Lincoln Murphy, Sixteen Ventures, "Desired Outcome" | Primary | `onboarding-plan`, `account-plan`, `expansion-case`: success is the customer's outcome, not their happiness |
| Renewal timeline and risk status | Gainsight Community, renewal playbooks and forecasting posts | Practitioner | `renewal-runway`: 90 day trigger with 60 and 30 checkpoints; risk visible at 90 days usually existed at 180 |
| Champion loss | ChurnZero champion playbook; Custify; Measured Success; Meritt | Practitioner | `champion-loss`: detect early, reach the replacement fast, multithread, learn the new decider from daily users |
| QBR and EBR | Gainsight QBR guide; SaaStr; thecscafe; Kapta and others | Practitioner | `business-review`: decisions not status, under an hour, outcome language, async for low-touch accounts |
| Onboarding | GitLab handbook (CSM onboarding); HubSpot; Guideless | Practitioner | `onboarding-plan`: define value before kickoff, kickoff within about 14 days, first cadence call within 30 |
| Escalation | Gainsight escalation and executive sponsor program; Supportbench; Gaintrace | Practitioner | `escalation`: named owner, scheduled holding updates, executive sponsor for repeat escalations |
| Handoff | Rocketlane; thecscafe; Inherit | Practitioner | `handoff`: written doc, internal sync in days, customer intro in a week, 30 day check, short templates |
| Health scores | Statisfy; BuildBetter; Gaintrace failure-mode write-ups | Practitioner | `health-score`: backtest against churn, 6 to 12 signals, baseline-relative, recalibrate |
| Retention metrics | ooligo; EnrichLabs; Distilled CS | Practitioner | `nrr-narrative`: report NRR and GRR together; the gap is the story |
| Coverage models | Customer Success Collective, coverage models and 2026 report | Survey | `coverage-model`: named, pooled and hybrid are all common; test against actual churn |
| AI adoption in CS | Bain (n=235, Aug 2025); Customer Success Collective 2026 | Survey | Design of the whole library: most teams are early, few have AI quality review |

## Things we deliberately did not use

- Single-vendor benchmarks for save rates, recovery rates, or the lift from running QBRs. They appear repeatedly with no traceable study.
- Churn "benchmarks" that mix monthly and annual, logo and revenue, gross and net.
- Any figure from a page that sells the product it measures.

## Honesty note

This version of the library was researched by searching and reading summaries and a subset of the primary pages. Some practitioner articles could not be opened in the environment used. If a skill states something that does not match your experience, that is a defect: open an issue.
