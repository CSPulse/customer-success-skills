# CSM Ramp

**A ramp plan for a new CSM and their manager: what to read, who to meet, what to own and when, and how both sides know the CSM is ramped.**

**What goes wrong without it:** Handing a new CSM the hardest accounts on day one, with no context and no time to listen first.

Part of [customer-success-skills](../../#readme).

## What it does

1. Find the clock before the curriculum
2. Split the book three ways
3. Write the weeks
4. Set what the CSM may not do yet
5. Build the manager checklist
6. Define ramped by outputs

## What you get

The answer comes first, then the reasoning. Every output ends with a list of what the skill could not check, so a gap shows up as a gap.

See a filled example for an invented company in [examples/sample.md](examples/sample.md).

Say things like:

- "ramp plan for a new CSM"
- "new CSM starts Monday"
- "30 60 90 for my new hire"
- "is my new CSM ramped"
- "what should my new CSM do in week one"
- "inherited a book as a new CSM"

## Who this is for

Written for team leads and heads of customer success. Included in: `cs-team-lead-toolkit`, `cs-leader-toolkit`.

## What it needs

Nothing is required. Paste what you have and it works with that. These inputs help most:

1. Start date, and whether the previous owner is still reachable (and for how many hours a week)
2. The book: account count, total ARR, the 10 largest, and every renewal or notice date inside 120 days
3. The CSM's background (new to CS, new to the product, new to the segment) and the manager's weekly time

Anything you leave out is listed under "What I could not check" instead of blocking the skill. No connector, file or other skill is needed.

## Rules it follows

- Do not hand over the fires on day one
- Week one is context, not customers
- Meet the biggest customers with the previous owner while that is still possible
- Ramped is a set of outputs, not a number of days
- Review early reads before the CSM acts on them
- Listening before committing is a rule, not caution
- Plan slack into holiday and leave weeks
