# Email craft for post-call emails

Load when drafting. If the user pasted samples of their own writing, match them over these defaults.

## Defaults

- Brevity is the point. Before each line ask what it is for. Before finishing, ask what can go.
- Write as "we" for the company. Use "I" only for a personal commitment.
- One or two sentences per paragraph. Bullets for actions, prose for context.
- Name dates and zones ("Tuesday 10 Nov, 15:00 GMT"), never "next week".
- Close forward. Address each ask to a named person, inline, not in a block at the bottom.
- Avoid "circle back", "touch base", "going forward", "hope this finds you well", "don't hesitate to reach out".

## Patterns

**Recap to the coordinator.** One specific opening line, then one line per open thread with state and owner. A thread with no owner is not a recap.

**Forwardable note** ("send me something I can pass to legal and security"). Nothing in it that the final reader does not need. Business case first, then the detail the specialist will test. Write for the person looking for a reason to decline. Offer documents on request rather than attaching all. End with one small, specific ask. Flag every product or security statement for confirmation.

**Intro nudge.** One paragraph, one ask, a line of thanks. If it needs a second paragraph it is not a nudge.

**Follow-up.** Answer in order: why now, what is outstanding, why it matters to them, what you need. Carry something new (a date moved, work you took off their plate). "Just checking in" answers nothing.

**Internal note.** Shorthand is fine. Lead with what the recipient needs, include the thing that did not come up but should have, end with a real question.

## Flags (under the draft, never in the body)

- **Product and security claims:** say it is the user's understanding and who should confirm. One wrong bullet in a security note ends the motion.
- **Commercial terms:** check against the signed agreement, not the call.
- **Ownership:** another person told the customer they would come back on this.
- **Missing data:** address, slot, spelling. Never fill silently.
