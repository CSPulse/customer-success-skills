# Usage Email

**A monthly note to the champion, drafted from pasted usage numbers, that they can forward without editing. Draft only, you sends.**

**What goes wrong without it:** A usage report that scolds. It lists unused seats as a failing, embarrasses a user in front of their boss and gets ignored.

Part of [customer-success-skills](../../#readme).

## What it does

1. Fix the measure
2. Call the month
3. Find the people story
4. Choose one recommendation and one ask
5. Keep quiet users and unused seats in aggregate
6. Draft in under 150 words
7. Write the internal notes

## What you get

The answer comes first, then the reasoning. Every output ends with a list of what the skill could not check, so a gap shows up as a gap.

See a filled example for an invented company in [examples/sample.md](examples/sample.md).

Say things like:

- "usage email"
- "monthly usage email"
- "send them their numbers"
- "usage recap for my champion"
- "how are they using it this month"
- "turn these usage numbers into an email"

## Who this is for

Written for CSMs. Included in: `csm-toolkit`.

## What it needs

Nothing is required. Paste what you have and it works with that. These inputs help most:

1. This month against last: active users, total actions, seats bought
2. The top three users, anyone who went quiet, any team with no use at all
3. The champion's name, and one line of how you writes (or "write plainly")

Anything you leave out is listed under "What I could not check" instead of blocking the skill. No connector, file or other skill is needed.

## Rules it follows

- Lead with their outcome and their people
- Thank named users for what they did, never rank the rest
- Unused seats are an offer to help, never a scolding
- One recommendation, one ask
- If usage is bad, say so plainly and bring a plan
- Never explain a movement you did not observe
- Say when the numbers are partial
- Counts name their measure
