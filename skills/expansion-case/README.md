# Expansion Case

**A one-page case the champion can defend alone, in their words, against other requests for the same money.**

**What goes wrong without it:** The deck that dies in an inbox because the champion could not defend slide four when their CFO pushed back.

Part of [customer-success-skills](../../#readme).

## What it does

1. Earn the ask
2. Find the anchor
3. Follow the money
4. Write three numbers in their units
5. Size a first step
6. Pre-answer the objections
7. Write it for the room you will not be in

## What you get

The answer comes first, then the reasoning. Every output ends with a list of what the skill could not check, so a gap shows up as a gap.

See a filled example for an invented company in [examples/sample.md](examples/sample.md).

Say things like:

- "expansion case"
- "they want to add seats"
- "second department"
- "build the business case for"
- "how do I get budget for"
- "my champion needs to sell this internally"
- "upsell"

## Who this is for

Written for CSMs. Included in: `csm-toolkit`.

## What it needs

Nothing is required. Paste what you have and it works with that. These inputs help most:

1. Account, current scope and value, and what they want to expand into
2. Who would sponsor it, and whose budget it would come from
3. What the first deployment has produced, in the customer's measure

Anything you leave out is listed under "What I could not check" instead of blocking the skill. No connector, file or other skill is needed.

## Rules it follows

- Do not ask on a deployment that is not working
- An unanchored case competes with every improvement
- When the money is another department's, the champion is a route, not the buyer
- A case that fits no committee fits no calendar
- Do not hand a champion optimistic numbers
- Show the implementation effort
- Answer these six in the document
- Do not bundle it into the renewal by accident
