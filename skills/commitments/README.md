# Commitments

**A two-sided ledger of what was promised, by whom and by when, from a pasted transcript or thread, so nothing owed slips and nothing owed to you goes unchased.**

**What goes wrong without it:** A promise made on a call that nobody writes down. Both sides forget it until the customer remembers it at renewal.

Part of [customer-success-skills](../../#readme).

## What it does

1. Find the binding sentences
2. Sort the rest
3. Resolve every date from the call date
4. Carry in older promises
5. Set status as of today
6. Pick three to close first
7. Draft one short nudge

## What you get

The answer comes first, then the reasoning. Every output ends with a list of what the skill could not check, so a gap shows up as a gap.

See a filled example for an invented company in [examples/sample.md](examples/sample.md).

Say things like:

- "commitments ledger"
- "who promised what"
- "what did we agree to"
- "what are they on the hook for"
- "pull the promises out of this thread"
- "log the action items from this call"

## Who this is for

Written for CSMs and team leads. Included in: `csm-toolkit`, `cs-team-lead-toolkit`.

## What it needs

Nothing is required. Paste what you have and it works with that. These inputs help most:

- Whatever you know about the situation

Anything you leave out is listed under "What I could not check" instead of blocking the skill. No connector, file or other skill is needed.

## Rules it follows

- A maybe is not a promise
- Never record a promise nobody made
- Implied dates get confirmed, not trusted
- Age is a relationship cost
- Slip out loud
- Their overdue promise is leverage and a signal
- Product, price, security and legal items are "verify before confirming"
- Every count names its measure
