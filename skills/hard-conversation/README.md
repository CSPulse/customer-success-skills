# Hard Conversation

**The two sentences to say first, the limits of what to offer, and the record to send the same day.**

**What goes wrong without it:** Three paragraphs of context and thanks before the news. The customer knew by the second sentence, waited for the rest and trusts the delivery less than the news.

Part of [customer-success-skills](../../#readme).

## What it does

1. Decide timing
2. Sort three lists
3. Write the opening two sentences literally
4. Set the ladder
5. Write one line for each reaction
6. Pick channel and room
7. Draft the same-day record

## What you get

The answer comes first, then the reasoning. Every output ends with a list of what the skill could not check, so a gap shows up as a gap.

See a filled example for an invented company in [examples/sample.md](examples/sample.md).

Say things like:

- "how do I tell them"
- "I have to tell the customer"
- "we missed the date"
- "I have been putting this off"
- "they are going to be angry"
- "how do I break this to"

## Who this is for

Written for CSMs. Included in: `csm-toolkit`.

## What it needs

Nothing is required. Paste what you have and it works with that. These inputs help most:

1. What happened (or will happen) and what was promised, ideally in the customer's own written words
2. Who is affected and who knows already
3. What you can offer without asking anyone

Anything you leave out is listed under "What I could not check" instead of blocking the skill. No connector, file or other skill is needed.

## Rules it follows

- Say the news in sentence one
- Own exactly the part that is yours
- Apology before explanation, and explanation only when asked
- Fix the concessions before the call
- Give the reason once
- Silence after the news is correct
- Cold is not fine
- No theory of cause
