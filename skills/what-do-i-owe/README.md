# What Do I Owe

**Paste the unread threads, Slack mentions and open tasks. Get a ranked list of what you owe, who is waiting on you, who owes you, and a draft for each reply that needs one.**

**What goes wrong without it:** Answering whatever arrived last. The email from the signer who has waited since Monday sits under newer, smaller requests.

Part of [customer-success-skills](../../#readme).

## What it does

1. Read each item as a debt
2. Split promise from request
3. Rank by consequence, then by who is waiting
4. Bucket every item once
5. Flag commitment risk
6. Draft one reply per thread that needs one,
7. Batch the low-stakes replies

## What you get

The answer comes first, then the reasoning. Every output ends with a list of what the skill could not check, so a gap shows up as a gap.

See a filled example for an invented company in [examples/sample.md](examples/sample.md).

Say things like:

- "what do I owe"
- "who is waiting on me"
- "my inbox is a mess"
- "what do I need to reply to"
- "rank my replies"
- "what am I behind on"

## Who this is for

Written for CSMs. Included in: `csm-toolkit`.

## What it needs

Nothing is required. Paste what you have and it works with that. These inputs help most:

1. The threads and mentions, with the date and time of the last message in each
2. Open tasks and anything you promised in writing this week
3. Today's calendar, if you wants the list fitted around meetings

Anything you leave out is listed under "What I could not check" instead of blocking the skill. No connector, file or other skill is needed.

## Rules it follows

- A promise outranks a request
- Rank by who is waiting, not who is loud
- Name the measure on every count
- A customer who already chased goes up a bucket
- Do not reply to everything
- Do not ack a deadline you cannot meet
- Internal blockers go first when they gate a customer reply
- Never invent a detail
