# Account Plan

**A durable plan for one account that someone inheriting it in month four could read and know what was promised, by whom, and what should be true by when.**

**What goes wrong without it:** A plan full of activity with no measure, no owner on the customer side and no way to be wrong. A plan that cannot fail never gets achieved.

Part of [customer-success-skills](../../#readme).

## What it does

1. Write one or two objectives in the customer's words
2. If no measure exists, say so first
3. State where it stands in four lines
4. Summarize the people
5. Write 3 to 5 actions
6. Give every risk a trigger
7. Set review triggers, not just a cadence

## What you get

The answer comes first, then the reasoning. Every output ends with a list of what the skill could not check, so a gap shows up as a gap.

See a filled example for an invented company in [examples/sample.md](examples/sample.md).

Say things like:

- "account plan"
- "strategic account plan"
- "twelve month plan"
- "what is our plan for [account]"
- "plan for this account"
- "write up the account strategy"

## Who this is for

Written for CSMs. Included in: `csm-toolkit`.

## What it needs

Nothing is required. Paste what you have and it works with that. These inputs help most:

1. Account, ARR, renewal date, notice period
2. What the customer says they are trying to achieve, in their words, and who said it
3. Who decides, who champions, and who has not been met

Anything you leave out is listed under "What I could not check" instead of blocking the skill. No connector, file or other skill is needed.

## Rules it follows

- A plan with no measure cannot be failed, so it is never achieved
- A measure without a baseline cannot show movement
- An objective nobody on their side owns is yours
- Five actions or fewer
- Actions that are all ours mean the customer never agreed
- A risk without a trigger is a worry
- Date everything and attribute commitments
- Not every account earns a plan
