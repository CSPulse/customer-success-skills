# Team Rollup

**Paste the CSMs' updates and your own notes. Get the one page your leadership reads, with the asks at the top and nothing in it they will first hear about somewhere else.**

**What goes wrong without it:** A summary that hides a risk until it is too big to hide. Leadership first hears about it when the renewal is lost.

Part of [customer-success-skills](../../#readme).

## What it does

1. Inventory the inputs
2. Find what needs leadership
3. Write the week in three lines
4. Build the figures
5. List risks that need help
6. List wins and commitments
7. Finish with what is unverified,

## What you get

The answer comes first, then the reasoning. Every output ends with a list of what the skill could not check, so a gap shows up as a gap.

See a filled example for an invented company in [examples/sample.md](examples/sample.md).

Say things like:

- "team rollup"
- "weekly roll-up for my VP"
- "what do I send upward"
- "summarize my CSMs' updates"
- "leadership summary for the week"
- "my director wants the team status"

## Who this is for

Written for team leads and heads of customer success. Included in: `cs-team-lead-toolkit`, `cs-leader-toolkit`.

## What it needs

Nothing is required. Paste what you have and it works with that. These inputs help most:

1. Each CSM's update, rough is fine, plus the lead's own notes
2. Book ARR, account count, and last week's roll-up if there is one (so change shows)
3. Renewals in the next 90 days with ARR and date, and which are flagged

Anything you leave out is listed under "What I could not check" instead of blocking the skill. No connector, file or other skill is needed.

## Rules it follows

- Asks first
- No surprises
- Do not hide a bad week
- Use the scorecard's definitions
- Every count names its measure
- Separate what the CSM said from what the lead reads
- Credit people by name and fairly
- A missing update is a finding
