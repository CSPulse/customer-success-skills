# Portfolio Review

**A review plan that finds which of a team's account reads are not supported by evidence, before they reach a forecast.**

**What goes wrong without it:** The review that turns into a working session on the first account raised. Everyone leaves feeling it was useful, and most of the reads go unexamined.

Part of [customer-success-skills](../../#readme).

## What it does

1. Name the meeting
2. Draw the sample by rule
3. Test every sampled read on paper
4. Scan all reads for tells,
5. Look across the book
6. Write the run of show
7. Close on changes

## What you get

The answer comes first, then the reasoning. Every output ends with a list of what the skill could not check, so a gap shows up as a gap.

See a filled example for an invented company in [examples/sample.md](examples/sample.md).

Say things like:

- "portfolio review"
- "review my team's reads"
- "everything is green and I do not believe it"
- "someone is sandbagging"
- "calibrate my team"
- "how do I run the forecast call"

## Who this is for

Written for team leads and heads of customer success. Included in: `cs-team-lead-toolkit`, `cs-leader-toolkit`.

## What it needs

Nothing is required. Paste what you have and it works with that. These inputs help most:

1. The reads: account, ARR, the CSM's call (green, likely, at risk) and their reason, ideally with last logged touch.
2. Meeting length and team size.
3. What you suspects: sandbagging, happy ears, or just "I do not believe it".

Anything you leave out is listed under "What I could not check" instead of blocking the skill. No connector, file or other skill is needed.

## Rules it follows

- Review reads, not accounts
- Draw the random five by rule
- Ask the same four questions in the same order
- Pull green accounts with no logged touch, every time
- Check the system before the person
- Never downgrade a read in front of peers as criticism
- Coaching in front of peers is a performance
- Take an action off someone's plate
