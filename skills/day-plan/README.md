# Day Plan

**Paste today's meetings, what you owe and your top priorities. Get a timeline that fits the day, a visible buffer, and what drops if the day overruns.**

**What goes wrong without it:** A day planned around the calendar that filled itself. Recaps happen at midnight, prep gets skipped and the one hard task slides to tomorrow.

Part of [customer-success-skills](../../#readme).

## What it does

1. Date the day by its start
2. Fix the immovable
3. Scale prep to stakes
4. Place a recap after every external call,
5. Put the hardest or most avoided task first,
6. Fill the gaps in this order
7. Count honestly

## What you get

The answer comes first, then the reasoning. Every output ends with a list of what the skill could not check, so a gap shows up as a gap.

See a filled example for an invented company in [examples/sample.md](examples/sample.md).

Say things like:

- "plan my day"
- "build my day around these meetings"
- "when do I do the recaps"
- "I have back to back calls today"
- "fit my replies around my calendar"
- "shut down time"

## Who this is for

Written for CSMs. Included in: `csm-toolkit`.

## What it needs

Nothing is required. Paste what you have and it works with that. These inputs help most:

1. Meetings today with start, end and who is on them (mark which are external)
2. Working hours, time zone, and personal blocks (lunch, school run, gym)
3. The owe list or the top 1 to 3 things that must move today

Anything you leave out is listed under "What I could not check" instead of blocking the skill. No connector, file or other skill is needed.

## Rules it follows

- Recaps within 30 minutes, not "later"
- Prep is scaled to what can go wrong
- No more than three meetings back to back without a reset
- A plan with no slack breaks by mid-day
- Say overfull out loud
- The hardest task goes before the inbox
- Protect one deep block and say what it is for
- Time zones are where plans fail
