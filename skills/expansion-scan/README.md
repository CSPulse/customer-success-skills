# Expansion Scan

**Paste the book. Get the few accounts worth an expansion conversation, ranked, sized conservatively, with the first conversation written for the best one.**

**What goes wrong without it:** Chasing the biggest account for an upsell while a smaller one that already asked for more goes unnoticed, or expanding a deployment that is not working.

Part of [customer-success-skills](../../#readme).

## What it does

1. Gate first
2. List the signals
3. Set confidence
4. Size from their side
5. Rank
6. Write the first conversation for the top account
7. Say how to log it

## What you get

The answer comes first, then the reasoning. Every output ends with a list of what the skill could not check, so a gap shows up as a gap.

See a filled example for an invented company in [examples/sample.md](examples/sample.md).

Say things like:

- "scan my book for expansion"
- "where is the upsell hiding"
- "who is ready to expand"
- "rank my expansion opportunities"
- "which accounts are outgrowing their plan"
- "seats are nearly full"

## Who this is for

Written for CSMs and heads of customer success. Included in: `csm-toolkit`, `cs-leader-toolkit`.

## What it needs

Nothing is required. Paste what you have and it works with that. These inputs help most:

1. The list: account, ARR, renewal date and notice period, seats bought and in use (or whatever unit you price on)
2. Usage movement over the last 30 to 60 days, plus health or open issues per account
3. Anything customers said: a new team asking, a new use case, a leader mentioning the product

Anything you leave out is listed under "What I could not check" instead of blocking the skill. No connector, file or other skill is needed.

## Rules it follows

- Expand only what is working
- A signal is a prompt, not an opportunity
- Rank by confidence times size times timing
- Size the customer's cost of the problem, not the price list
- Full seats are capacity, not demand
- Mind the notice date
- Hand over without burning the relationship
- Log estimates as estimates
