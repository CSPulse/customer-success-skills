# Playbook Builder

**A one-page play for a recurring situation, so every CSM on the team responds the same way and the response can be improved.**

**What goes wrong without it:** A save that depends on which CSM owns the account. The same situation gets a different response every time because the play lives in one person's head.

Part of [customer-success-skills](../../#readme).

## What it does

1. Write the trigger as something observable
2. Name one owner by role, and a backup
3. Write four to seven steps
4. Write exit criteria in both directions
5. Draft two or three message templates
6. Choose three measures
7. Test it on one real past case

## What you get

The answer comes first, then the reasoning. Every output ends with a list of what the skill could not check, so a gap shows up as a gap.

See a filled example for an invented company in [examples/sample.md](examples/sample.md).

Say things like:

- "build a playbook"
- "turn this into a play"
- "we keep handling this differently"
- "write the play for"
- "make this repeatable"
- "standardize our response to"

## Who this is for

Written for team leads and heads of customer success. Included in: `cs-team-lead-toolkit`, `cs-leader-toolkit`.

## What it needs

Nothing is required. Paste what you have and it works with that. These inputs help most:

1. The situation, and roughly how often it happens ("new accounts go quiet after kickoff, a few a month").
2. What the team does today and where it goes wrong or differs by person.
3. Who would own the play, and how big the team is.

Anything you leave out is listed under "What I could not check" instead of blocking the skill. No connector, file or other skill is needed.

## Rules it follows

- One trigger, one play
- The trigger must be observable by someone who did not follow the account
- The play ends
- Say what not to do
- Write the response into the account record
- Keep it to one page
- Templates are drafts, not scripts
- Do not build a play for a one-off
