# Sales Handoff

**Turn a signed deal and whatever the AE wrote into an intake the CSM can run from, plus the questions that close the gaps before the customer finds them.**

**What goes wrong without it:** The promises made during the sale that never reached a system, found by the customer in the first month.

Part of [customer-success-skills](../../#readme).

## What it does

1. Check what already exists
2. Why they bought, in their words
3. Build the promise ledger
4. Map the people
5. Record the rest
6. Write the questions for the AE
7. Plan the first week and set the sign-off

## What you get

The answer comes first, then the reasoning. Every output ends with a list of what the skill could not check, so a gap shows up as a gap.

See a filled example for an invented company in [examples/sample.md](examples/sample.md).

Say things like:

- "closed-won handoff"
- "the AE just closed"
- "sales handoff"
- "what did sales promise"
- "intake from the AE"
- "questions for the AE"

## Who this is for

Written for CSMs, team leads and heads of customer success. Included in: `csm-toolkit`, `cs-team-lead-toolkit`, `cs-leader-toolkit`.

## What it needs

Nothing is required. Paste what you have and it works with that. These inputs help most:

1. The AE's handoff notes, deal summary or opportunity notes
2. The order form or contract terms: units, price, term, start, renewal and notice, special clauses
3. Anything the customer wrote during the sale (RFP answers, emails, call notes)

Anything you leave out is listed under "What I could not check" instead of blocking the skill. No connector, file or other skill is needed.

## Rules it follows

- Trust the paper over the person
- Separate told from true
- Name the economic buyer even if nobody has met them
- Meet the customer within a week
- Capture the baseline now
- Record why the competitor lost and what they offered
- Never restart or duplicate
- Done means the CSM says done
