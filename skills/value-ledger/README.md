# Value Ledger

**What the customer set out to achieve and the proof they are getting it, in one place the QBR, the renewal read and the sponsor's own business case can all draw from.**

**What goes wrong without it:** Reaching renewal with a happy champion and no proof. When finance asks what the product delivered, nobody has the number.

Part of [customer-success-skills](../../#readme).

## What it does

1. Write each goal in the customer's measure,
2. Compute progress
3. Log proof
4. Age every row
5. Attribute honestly
6. Price it only from the customer's inputs,
7. End with one ask

## What you get

The answer comes first, then the reasoning. Every output ends with a list of what the skill could not check, so a gap shows up as a gap.

See a filled example for an invented company in [examples/sample.md](examples/sample.md).

Say things like:

- "value ledger"
- "what has the customer actually achieved"
- "do we have proof of value"
- "capture their success goals"
- "what is our evidence of ROI"
- "log this win"

## Who this is for

Written for CSMs. Included in: `csm-toolkit`.

## What it needs

Nothing is required. Paste what you have and it works with that. These inputs help most:

1. What the customer said they bought this to achieve, in their words
2. Any result they have mentioned since: a number, a quote, a milestone
3. The renewal date

Anything you leave out is listed under "What I could not check" instead of blocking the skill. No connector, file or other skill is needed.

## Rules it follows

- Value lives in the customer's measure
- No baseline, no proof
- Customer-stated beats vendor-computed
- Evidence ages
- Say what else changed
- Anything proposed is a proposal
- Get the champion to say it in writing
- Never invent a number
