# Exec Conversation

**A short script for a senior customer who has no context and will leave early: first sentence, one ask, two questions, follow-up.**

**What goes wrong without it:** The product tour. Fifteen minutes explaining the platform to someone who does not care how it works, ending in a handshake and nothing else.

Part of [customer-success-skills](../../#readme).

## What it does

1. Find out why the meeting exists
2. Name what they are measured on
3. Write the first sentence
4. Pick one ask
5. Prepare two questions
6. Protect the champion
7. Plan eight minutes and four,

## What you get

The answer comes first, then the reasoning. Every output ends with a list of what the skill could not check, so a gap shows up as a gap.

See a filled example for an invented company in [examples/sample.md](examples/sample.md).

Say things like:

- "I have fifteen minutes with their CFO"
- "meeting my champion's boss"
- "exec sponsor meeting"
- "new VP just joined"
- "what do I say to a C-level"

## Who this is for

Written for CSMs. Included in: `csm-toolkit`.

## What it needs

Nothing is required. Paste what you have and it works with that. These inputs help most:

1. Their title, and who set up the meeting (the champion, your side, or the executive)
2. What the account has delivered, in any number the customer recognizes
3. What you needs from this person

Anything you leave out is listed under "What I could not check" instead of blocking the skill. No connector, file or other skill is needed.

## Rules it follows

- Lead with the conclusion
- One ask
- No ask is also a failure
- Drop the vendor vocabulary
- Plan for eight minutes
- Never raise anything the champion has not seen,
- A contradiction with the champion is the most valuable thing said
- Finish early
