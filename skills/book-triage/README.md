# Book Triage

**Paste the account list. Get this week's short list, the one quiet account to check on purpose, what you are not touching, and how the days run.**

**What goes wrong without it:** A week spent on the accounts that asked. Whoever emails loudest is rarely the biggest risk or the biggest value, and the quiet accounts where the danger sits get nothing.

Part of [customer-success-skills](../../#readme).

## What it does

1. Rank by movement, not state
2. Find the binding date
3. Ask "if I spend four hours here, does anything move?"
4. Pick the silent account on purpose
5. Weight by value last
6. Write the not-this-week list
7. Shape Monday to Friday

## What you get

The answer comes first, then the reasoning. Every output ends with a list of what the skill could not check, so a gap shows up as a gap.

See a filled example for an invented company in [examples/sample.md](examples/sample.md).

Say things like:

- "triage my book"
- "which accounts this week"
- "plan my week"
- "my book is too big"
- "who needs me this week"
- "I cannot cover them all"

## Who this is for

Written for CSMs and team leads. Included in: `csm-toolkit`, `cs-team-lead-toolkit`.

## What it needs

Nothing is required. Paste what you have and it works with that. These inputs help most:

1. The list: account, ARR, renewal date, notice period
2. Last real contact per account (a human reply or a call, not an automated email)
3. Anything that moved in the last 2 to 4 weeks that you already knows about

Anything you leave out is listed under "What I could not check" instead of blocking the skill. No connector, file or other skill is needed.

## Rules it follows

- Movement beats color and size
- The notice date is the deadline
- Nobody emailing is not the same as nothing wrong
- Name the accounts where nothing can move
- A fifth of a week unscheduled is not slack, it is absorption
- An account on the list three weeks running with nothing moving is a signal about the action,
- A tail that is on the not-this-week list every week is a coverage problem
- Triage weekly
