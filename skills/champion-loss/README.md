# Champion Loss

**A first-10-days plan and three drafts for the moment the person who carried the account is gone, leaving, or quiet.**

**What goes wrong without it:** Finding out your sponsor left when the renewal arrives. The new owner forms a view of your product from someone else's story before you have spoken to them.

Part of [customer-success-skills](../../#readme).

## What it does

1. Confirm what happened, tag it
2. Find the replacement and the real decider
3. Reach each stakeholder with a message framed so they can answer either way
4. Re-establish value per role
5. Re-multithread
6. Tie it to the clock
7. Write the response down

## What you get

The answer comes first, then the reasoning. Every output ends with a list of what the skill could not check, so a gap shows up as a gap.

See a filled example for an invented company in [examples/sample.md](examples/sample.md).

Say things like:

- "my champion left"
- "sponsor left"
- "our contact changed roles"
- "my sponsor went dark"
- "new owner on the account"
- "they deactivated their account"

## Who this is for

Written for CSMs. Included in: `csm-toolkit`.

## What it needs

Nothing is required. Paste what you have and it works with that. These inputs help most:

1. Account, ARR, renewal date and notice period
2. Who left, went quiet or changed roles, how you found out, and what they owned
3. Anyone else you knows there, and who might replace them

Anything you leave out is listed under "What I could not check" instead of blocking the skill. No connector, file or other skill is needed.

## Rules it follows

- Detect early
- Reach the replacement before they form a view
- The lost champion's optimism does not transfer
- Frame every message so no answer is bad news
- Learn the new decider from daily users
- Do not forward the exit story as fact
- Never send, never post
