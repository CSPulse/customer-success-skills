# Causes of low adoption

Load this at step 2. Pick the primary cause from the evidence, not from the first story you were told. Every threshold is a common default, set your own.

| Cause | What you would see | What to ask | Fix | Owner |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Never started | Seats issued, near-zero logins since day one, no admin activity | "Who was meant to start, and what stopped them?" | Short path to first result in one week, a named launch owner | Ours to build, theirs to launch |
| Started and stalled | Early spike, then a decline after week 3 or 4 | "What did they do in week one that they no longer do?" | Find the step where they dropped, remove that friction, re-launch with one use case | Joint |
| One team only | One team active, others near zero, usage steady | "Why did that team adopt and the others not?" | Copy what that team did (a manager, a workflow) into the next team | Theirs (managers), ours (playbook) |
| Wrong people licensed | Active users cluster in a few roles, whole roles at zero | "What does this role do all day, and where would this fit?" | Reassign or release the seats, do not train the role | Theirs to decide, ours to raise |
| Product fit gap | Users try it, then say it cannot do a step in their job | "What do you do instead, and how often?" | Re-scope the use case, log the gap with product, set an honest expectation | Ours |
| Champion gone | Heaviest user quiet, no one owns the rollout, tickets stop | "Who owns this now?" | Name a new owner first, then plan (see `champion-loss`) | Theirs and ours |
| Integration broken | Usage fell off on a date, tickets or workarounds mention a sync or login | "What changed on that date?" | Fix or replace the integration, then measure again | Ours (support), theirs (IT) |
| Change-management failure | Tool works, people are told to use it, the old way is still allowed | "Is the old process still available, and who checks?" | Sponsor sets a dated cutover and retires the old way for the cohort | Theirs (sponsor) |

Quick separators:

- Spike then decline points to stalled. Flat near zero points to never started.
- A drop on one date points to an integration or a person. A slow slide points to fit or change management.
- If heavy users vanish together, look for a person or a change on their side before blaming the product.
- Two causes can both be true. Fix the one that unblocks the other first: owner, then integration, then fit, then training.
