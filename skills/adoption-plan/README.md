# Adoption Plan

**Paste the usage you have. Get the cause of low usage, the one group to move first, and a 30 day plan that ends in a test, not a training invite.**

**What goes wrong without it:** Offering training to every account with unused seats, whatever the cause. A stalled rollout, a broken integration and the wrong people holding licenses each need a different fix.

Part of [customer-success-skills](../../#readme).

## What it does

1. Pin the measure
2. Diagnose one primary cause
3. Pick the cohort that can move first
4. Define success as a behavior with a date
5. Plan 30 days by cause
6. Decide the seat conversation
7. Write the fallback

## What you get

The answer comes first, then the reasoning. Every output ends with a list of what the skill could not check, so a gap shows up as a gap.

See a filled example for an invented company in [examples/sample.md](examples/sample.md).

Say things like:

- "low adoption"
- "they are not using it"
- "usage has stalled"
- "shelfware"
- "seats sitting idle"
- "adoption plan"

## Who this is for

Written for CSMs and team leads. Included in: `csm-toolkit`, `cs-team-lead-toolkit`.

## What it needs

Nothing is required. Paste what you have and it works with that. These inputs help most:

1. Seats or licenses bought against active, split by team or role if known, and what "active" means in the export (a login, or a real action)
2. What they bought it to do, and who owns that outcome on their side
3. What changed when usage stopped growing (a person left, a launch slipped, nothing known)

Anything you leave out is listed under "What I could not check" instead of blocking the skill. No connector, file or other skill is needed.

## Rules it follows

- Count people doing the work, not logins
- "Offer training" fixes one cause in eight
- Breadth before depth
- Move the cohort with one removable blocker
- Raise unused seats yourself
- A gone champion or a broken integration comes first
- Check the calendar before calling it stalled
- Do not plan around a fit gap
