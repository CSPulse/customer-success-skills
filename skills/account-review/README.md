# Account Review

**One page per account per month, in the same shape every time, so change is visible and one decision gets made.**

**What goes wrong without it:** A monthly review that looks different every time, so nobody can see what changed. The sections skipped first, proof of value and open promises, are the ones that matter most.

Part of [customer-success-skills](../../#readme).

## What it does

1. Set the clock
2. Change first
3. Show health in parts,
4. Goals and proof
5. People
6. Commitments
7. Risks and expansion
8. Next 30 days

## What you get

The answer comes first, then the reasoning. Every output ends with a list of what the skill could not check, so a gap shows up as a gap.

See a filled example for an invented company in [examples/sample.md](examples/sample.md).

Say things like:

- "account on a page"
- "monthly account review"
- "account review for"
- "one page on [account]"
- "what changed on this account since last month"
- "run my monthly review"

## Who this is for

Written for CSMs and team leads. Included in: `csm-toolkit`, `cs-team-lead-toolkit`.

## What it needs

Nothing is required. Paste what you have and it works with that. These inputs help most:

1. Account, ARR, renewal date, notice period
2. What happened this month: calls, people, usage, tickets, requests
3. Last month's review, or "none"

Anything you leave out is listed under "What I could not check" instead of blocking the skill. No connector, file or other skill is needed.

## Rules it follows

- Same shape every month
- One decision per review
- Health stays in parts
- Proof and commitments cannot be skipped
- Name the measure on every count
- Silence from a decider outranks good usage
- A signal is not an opportunity
- List what you could not check
