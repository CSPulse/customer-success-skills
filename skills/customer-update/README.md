# Customer Update

**You walks away with a segmented send plan and a broadcast that tells each reader in two lines whether it affects them and whether to act.**

**What goes wrong without it:** An update written from your side of the glass. The change that affects the customer sits in the middle of a paragraph about your roadmap.

Part of [customer-success-skills](../../#readme).

## What it does

1. Segment by impact, not by mailing list
2. Write the subject line first and make it the news
3. Lead with three lines
4. Split known from unknown
5. Make it survive a forward
6. Set the reply plan
7. Pick the order of sends

## What you get

The answer comes first, then the reasoning. Every output ends with a list of what the skill could not check, so a gap shows up as a gap.

See a filled example for an invented company in [examples/sample.md](examples/sample.md).

Say things like:

- "customer update"
- "notify customers"
- "announcement to customers"
- "end of life notice"
- "maintenance window"
- "outage notice"
- "tell them about the delay"

## Who this is for

Written for CSMs. Included in: `csm-toolkit`.

## What it needs

Nothing is required. Paste what you have and it works with that. These inputs help most:

1. What is changing, and the date
2. Which customers it touches and how many (a rough split is fine)
3. Anything contractual: notice period, credits, security or legal review

Anything you leave out is listed under "What I could not check" instead of blocking the skill. No connector, file or other skill is needed.

## Rules it follows

- One message to the whole list fails twice
- "No action is needed" is load-bearing
- A next-update date is a promise
- No cause before it is established
- No credits or concessions in a broadcast
- Do not apologize for a decision, only for harm
- A CSM change is a relationship event
- Brief support before it sends
