# Handoff

**A handover the receiver can run the account from: either the sender's brain emptied onto one page, or an inherited account audited down to what is actually known.**

**What goes wrong without it:** The account that arrives as a folder, with everything documented and nothing that matters, or the account that leaves with its owner and the promises with it.

Part of [customer-success-skills](../../#readme).

## What it does

1. Set the clock
2. OUTGOING: force out the four
3. OUTGOING: list open promises and the last five threads and meetings
4. OUTGOING: the receiver decides when it is done
5. INCOMING: find the original promise
6. INCOMING: mark every claim handed over
7. INCOMING: plan the first 14 days, then the 30 day check

## What you get

The answer comes first, then the reasoning. Every output ends with a list of what the skill could not check, so a gap shows up as a gap.

See a filled example for an invented company in [examples/sample.md](examples/sample.md).

Say things like:

- "handoff"
- "I am leaving this account"
- "hand over my accounts"
- "I just inherited this account"
- "taking over the account"
- "get me up to speed on this account"

## Who this is for

Written for CSMs and team leads. Included in: `csm-toolkit`, `cs-team-lead-toolkit`.

## What it needs

Nothing is required. Paste what you have and it works with that. These inputs help most:

1. Account, ARR, renewal date, notice period, and which mode
2. Who is leaving and who is receiving, and the sender's last day
3. Whatever exists: CRM notes, the contract, recent emails or meeting notes

Anything you leave out is listed under "What I could not check" instead of blocking the skill. No connector, file or other skill is needed.

## Rules it follows

- The receiver declares it complete
- An undocumented promise is the renewal's real scorecard
- The sceptic is never in the CRM
- Folklore shapes the approach before anyone is met
- A new executive above the contact is the biggest find
- Access without context is a trap
- Do not hand over mid-escalation or inside a renewal window if the date can move
- Do not pretend in the first meeting
