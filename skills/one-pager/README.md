# One Pager

**You walks away with one page, in the sponsor's words, that a stranger can act on in forty seconds, and the covering line to send it.**

**What goes wrong without it:** The document the sponsor rewrites before forwarding it. Once they edit it, it stops sounding like them and you lose control of what their leadership reads.

Part of [customer-success-skills](../../#readme).

## What it does

1. Name the document type
2. Write the first three lines
3. Use the forwarder's vocabulary
4. Add three or four short sections
5. Make every number theirs and checkable
6. Answer the one question the forwarder will get
7. Write the forwarder line

## What you get

The answer comes first, then the reasoning. Every output ends with a list of what the skill could not check, so a gap shows up as a gap.

See a filled example for an invented company in [examples/sample.md](examples/sample.md).

Say things like:

- "one pager"
- "something they can forward"
- "my sponsor needs to send this up"
- "summary for their boss"
- "leave-behind"
- "brief for their leadership"

## Who this is for

Written for CSMs. Included in: `csm-toolkit`.

## What it needs

Nothing is required. Paste what you have and it works with that. These inputs help most:

1. What should the reader know or decide
2. Who forwards it, and to whom (the reader was not in the room)
3. The sponsor's own words for the project and metrics

Anything you leave out is listed under "What I could not check" instead of blocking the skill. No connector, file or other skill is needed.

## Rules it follows

- The sponsor is the client
- Conclusion in the first three lines
- No logo, no branding
- Their words, not yours
- A number nobody can check is decoration
- Shrinking the font is avoiding the cut
- The forwarder line does more work than the page
- Ship editable text
