# Status Update

**Paste rough notes. Get the update you owe someone, led by outcomes, with the bad news stated plainly and the asks specific.**

**What goes wrong without it:** A weekly update that lists activity. The manager learns nothing about outcomes and hears the bad news for the first time at the forecast call.

Part of [customer-success-skills](../../#readme).

## What it does

1. Turn activity into outcomes
2. Sort facts from hope
3. Put the lowlights in, plainly,
4. Make every ask specific
5. Name the measure on every count
6. Cut to length
7. Close with the sources

## What you get

The answer comes first, then the reasoning. Every output ends with a list of what the skill could not check, so a gap shows up as a gap.

See a filled example for an invented company in [examples/sample.md](examples/sample.md).

Say things like:

- "write my standup"
- "weekly update for my manager"
- "end of day wrap"
- "what did I get done this week"
- "status update"
- "update for my boss"

## Who this is for

Written for CSMs and team leads. Included in: `csm-toolkit`, `cs-team-lead-toolkit`.

## What it needs

Nothing is required. Paste what you have and it works with that. These inputs help most:

1. Which update, and who reads it
2. The notes, in any state: bullets, a task list, call summaries
3. Anything that went wrong this period that the reader does not know yet

Anything you leave out is listed under "What I could not check" instead of blocking the skill. No connector, file or other skill is needed.

## Rules it follows

- Lead with outcomes, not activity
- Bad news now is cheaper than bad news later
- Asks are a name, an action and a date
- Separate what is true from what is hoped
- Every count names its measure
- Say what was not read
- Do not pad a quiet week
- Credit others by name
