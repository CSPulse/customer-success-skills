# Save Plan

**Turn an at-risk read into a 30 day plan with one owner, a checkpoint that can fail, and a landing you chose before the pressure started.**

**What goes wrong without it:** A save plan launched after the customer already chose a replacement, or one that opens with a discount before anyone has offered a fix.

Part of [customer-success-skills](../../#readme).

## What it does

1. Decide if there is a window
2. Name the mechanism and match the play
3. Set the people
4. Put the fix and one proof first, the price last
5. Pre-decide the landing
6. Write it by week
7. Log it

## What you get

The answer comes first, then the reasoning. Every output ends with a list of what the skill could not check, so a gap shows up as a gap.

See a filled example for an invented company in [examples/sample.md](examples/sample.md).

Say things like:

- "save plan"
- "save this account"
- "can we still save them"
- "30 day rescue"
- "they are leaning toward leaving"
- "turnaround plan for"

## Who this is for

Written for CSMs, team leads and heads of customer success. Included in: `csm-toolkit`, `cs-team-lead-toolkit`, `cs-leader-toolkit`.

## What it needs

Nothing is required. Paste what you have and it works with that. These inputs help most:

1. Account, ARR, renewal date, notice date, and what you has been told by whom
2. Who decides and whether you has met them
3. What your company can offer: executive time, price or term room, services hours

Anything you leave out is listed under "What I could not check" instead of blocking the skill. No connector, file or other skill is needed.

## Rules it follows

- No window, no plan
- Match the play to the mechanism
- Fix before price
- Protect the relationship with the decider, not only the champion
- One owner
- The day 15 checkpoint must be able to fail
- Choose the contraction in advance
- Never trade the unverified
