# Plays by mechanism

Load this at step 2. Match the play to the cause. Timings are a common default, set your own.

| Mechanism | What it looks like | Play | Do not |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Budget cut | "Everything is being reviewed", a finance owner appears | Protect the budget line: show cost avoided or revenue protected in finance's measure, offer a smaller scope or phased term, give the champion a number to defend | Argue for the full price before you know the target cut |
| Failed rollout | Low usage, blame on the tool, no owner | Fix the rollout first (see `adoption-plan`), get one team to a visible result, then the sponsor reviews it | Offer a discount to cover a deployment that does not work |
| Sponsor lost or reorganized | New owner, unanswered emails, nobody carries the value story | Earn a first meeting with the new owner through the old sponsor's intro, bring one page in the new owner's measure, ask what they need to see | Wait for them to find you, or restart with a feature tour |
| Competitor or price pressure | A quote from another vendor, a request for a comparison | Find what the alternative actually does for them, name the switching cost honestly, trade term for price | Cut price before you know what they are comparing |
| Service failure | Repeat tickets, a missed date, a named incident | Own it in writing, name a fix date, give a single accountable contact, hold a weekly update until closed (see `escalation`) | Send value slides while the problem is open |
| Product gap | A repeated "it cannot do X" | State what exists, what does not, and the workaround, log the gap, agree what would change their mind | Promise a roadmap date you cannot verify |
| Unknown | Quiet, vague, no reason given | Week 1 is a listening call with the decider and the daily users, no pitch | Pick a play before you have a mechanism |

Commercial options to decide before the first call, in the order to offer them: hold price for a longer term, phase the scope with a right to add back at the same unit price, move payment terms, trade a case study or reference, and last a time-limited reduction tied to a signed term. Each trades something. A pure discount trades nothing.
